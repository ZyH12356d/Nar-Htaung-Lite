A note on privacy.

This website has no account system and does not add advertising or analytics scripts. Website and download hosting providers may keep their own request logs.

In the current Android app, downloaded music and collection records are stored on your device. Searching, downloading, and fetching missing cover artwork connect to YouTube and its image services.

Removing the app or clearing its data can remove your locally saved library.
